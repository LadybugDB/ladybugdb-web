Graph database comparisons

Honest, engineer-first comparisons of LadybugDB — DuckDB for graphs, MIT-licensed — against the databases teams evaluate most. Ordered by importance.

One advantage shows up in every matchup: LadybugDB is portable native C++. It runs WASM in the browser, natively on iOS and Android, and on any architecture with a C compiler — so your graph runs on devices where server-bound rivals can't follow.
