why ladybug?

Small. Fast.
Lucky.

Ladybugs eat the pests and leave the garden alone. They also have excellent scent-based memory that lets them remember migration paths and trails left by their ancestors. LadybugDB does the same to your stack: one small embedded graph engine that removes servers, sidecars, and ops — and leaves the data.
